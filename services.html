Faq's

This is the first item Spider plant. Spider Plant is an easy-to-care-for indoor plant. It grows well in bright, indirect sunlight and needs moderate watering. It can also grow in low-light areas. This plant is a good choice for homes, offices, and beginners.

This is the second item Money Plant.Money Plant grows well in indirect sunlight and can adapt to different indoor conditions. Water it when the soil becomes slightly dry. It can be grown in both soil and water. Regular trimming helps maintain healthy growth.

This is the third item Aloe Vera. Aloe Vera requires less water compared with many other plants. Allow the soil to dry before watering it again. It prefers bright sunlight and well-drained soil. Avoid overwatering because it can damage the roots.

This is the third item Snake plant Yes, Snake Plant is an excellent indoor plant and is very easy to maintain. It can grow in low to bright indirect light. It needs little water and can tolerate dry conditions. It is suitable for homes, offices, and bedrooms.
