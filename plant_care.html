Plant Care💧

💧
Watering

Water your plants regularly according to their needs. Avoid overwatering and allow the soil to dry when required.

☀️
Sunlight

Provide the right amount of sunlight for healthy plant growth. Different plants require different light conditions.

🌱
Healthy Soil

Use good-quality and well-drained soil for your plants. Healthy soil provides nutrients for strong growth.

🌿
Fertilizer

Use suitable fertilizer when needed to provide essential nutrients and support healthy plant growth.

✂️
Pruning

Remove dry or damaged leaves and branches regularly. Pruning helps maintain the plant's healthy appearance.

🐛
Pest Control

Check plants regularly for pests and signs of damage. Early care helps keep plants healthy and growing well.
